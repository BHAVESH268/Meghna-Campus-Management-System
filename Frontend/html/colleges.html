SCHOOL

Shree Karsanbhai Jethidas Patel Sanskar Vidhyalay

Also known as the Meghna Chhatralaya Campus, Kadi, Mehsana, Gujarat.

01

General Overview

  • Medium: Both Gujarati and English medium education are available on the campus. The English-medium school is known as Shree Rajesh Kantilal Patel Sanskar English Medium School.
  • Standards: Education is provided from Nursery to Standard 12, including Science and Commerce streams.
  • Board: Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB).
  • Main Objective: To provide high-quality, value-based education at nominal and affordable fees, particularly for children from middle- and lower-middle-income families.
02

Special Features & Facilities

  • Digital Education: Advanced audio-visual and online systems are used to make difficult subjects easier to understand.
  • Infrastructure: Spacious classrooms with sufficient ventilation and lighting, along with air-conditioning (AC) facilities.
  • Personal Attention: Special diagnostic and remedial classes are conducted for students who need additional academic support.
  • Regular Assessment: Students' progress is evaluated through chapter-wise tests and unit tests.
  • Parent-Teacher Interaction: Regular Parent-Teacher Meetings are conducted to support students' overall development.
  • Activities: Every student is encouraged/required to participate in at least one sports or cultural activity.
03

School Information

NURSING

Shree Umiya Nursing College

Meghna Campus, Kadi

01

About the College

Shree Umiya Nursing College (Meghna Campus, Kadi) is a well-known private nursing college located in Kadi Taluka of Mehsana district, Gujarat. The college is managed by Swami Vivekanand Education Trust (SVET) and is affiliated with Hemchandracharya North Gujarat University (HNGU), Patan.

02

Courses Offered

GNM

General Nursing and Midwifery — 3-year diploma course.

Basic B.Sc. Nursing

4-year undergraduate degree course.

Post Basic B.Sc. Nursing

2-year degree course for students who have completed GNM.

03

Eligibility & Admission

Candidates should have passed Class 12 with at least 45% marks. Eligibility requirements may vary depending on the course.

The minimum age requirement is 17 years as of 31st December of the year of admission.

Admission may be based on the merit list and rules of the Gujarat Nursing Admission Committee or through the college-level admission process.
