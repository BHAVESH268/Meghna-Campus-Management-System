  • School Type: Co-educational, English-medium institution located in Kadi, Mehsana District, Gujarat.
  • Establishment & Governance: Founded in June 2019 and operated under the Swami Vivekanand Education Trust (SVET).
  • Primary Objective: To provide culture-oriented English-medium education at nominal fee rates.
  • Academic Streams: Affiliated with the Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB), serving students from Nursery through Grade 12.
  • Senior Secondary: Science Stream with PCM and PCB groups.
